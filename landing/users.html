HOW IT WORKS

A gesture becomes
a keyboard command.

  1. 01

    A camera sees the gesture

    A compatible camera captures the presenter’s hands and arms.

  2. 02

    Vinka recognises the movement

    Gesture software looks for deliberate directional movements and maps accepted gestures to commands.

  3. 03

    The USB receiver sends the command

    The receiver plugs into the presentation computer and sends keyboard commands to the active slide application.

THE CURRENT PROTOTYPE

What you need.

Vinka is a working prototype. A camera and a separate computer run the gesture recognition; the USB receiver connects to the computer showing your slides.

  • A compatible camera and gesture-recognition software
  • A separate computer to process the camera feed
  • A Vinka USB receiver and a shared local network
  • A slide application controlled by keyboard commands

No Vinka app on the presenter’s laptop. The recognition computer processes video locally. Only commands travel over the network to the receiver. Compatibility should be checked with your camera and presentation software.
