Gesture control for presentations

Use hand movements to control your slides without holding a remote or returning to your laptop.

Plug and present

In a prepared room, plug in the receiver and start presenting. No software installation or configuration is needed on your presentation laptop.

Configurable gestures

Choose the commands assigned to up, down, left and right movements using the browser-based settings.

How the prototype works

  1. 01

    A camera sees the gesture

    A compatible camera captures the presenter. Hands and arms need to remain visible to the camera.

  2. 02

    Vinka recognises the movement

    Software running on a separate computer recognises directional movements and sends accepted commands over the local network.

  3. 03

    The USB receiver sends the command

    The receiver plugs into the presentation computer and sends keyboard commands to the active slide application.

What the prototype needs

Vinka is a working prototype. A camera and a separate computer run the gesture recognition; the USB receiver connects to the computer showing your slides.

  • A compatible camera and gesture-recognition software
  • A separate computer to process the camera feed
  • A Vinka USB receiver and a shared local network
  • A slide application controlled by keyboard commands

What comes next

Today

The prototype supports configurable directional gestures. Recognition can miss movements or produce unintended commands, so reliability and camera placement still need further testing.

Our goal

Improve recognition and test it in presentation rooms, then explore integration with AV equipment. The goal is a room that is ready to use without presenters installing software or configuring gesture control themselves.
