THE OPPORTUNITY

Another way to control
the presentation.

Vinka is being developed as an optional gesture-control feature for camera manufacturers and room-solution providers. Teaching, training and presentation rooms are the intended settings.

Camera-based input

Use deliberate hand and arm movements as input to presentation control.

Configurable commands

Map recognised gestures to keyboard commands through the receiver’s keybinding editor.

USB keyboard output

The receiver sends commands to the presentation computer as a USB keyboard.

WHERE WE ARE

Working prototype.
Open to a pilot.

The current prototype processes camera video on a separate computer and sends commands over the local network to the receiver. Integration into commercial cameras or room systems is a proposed next step.

What we’d explore together

  • Camera and software compatibility
  • Recognition behaviour in real presentation rooms
  • Integration requirements and command mappings
  • Presenter experience and room setup
