An optional feature for AV systems

The concept is to integrate gesture control into an AV partner’s camera and room-system offerings. Together, we would determine how Vinka fits the partner’s hardware and software for teaching, training and meeting rooms.

Use the system’s camera

A compatible camera in the partner’s AV system could capture the presenter’s hand and arm movements without a separate dedicated camera.

Integrate gesture recognition

Recognition could run on compatible room hardware or in the cloud. The approach would be decided with the partner based on compatibility, cost and user needs.

Present without installing software

The goal is to deliver commands through the room system so presenters can connect their laptop and use gesture control without installing software.

From prototype to pilot

The working prototype turns camera input into presentation commands using a recognition computer and USB receiver. A pilot with an AV partner would test the experience in real rooms and explore how gesture control could be integrated into the partner’s systems.

What a pilot would address

  • Camera and system integration
  • Recognition accuracy and responsiveness
  • Command mappings and presentation software
  • Presenter experience, installation and support

Development roadmap

Our proposed path towards an optional feature for compatible AV systems.

  1. 01

    Current prototype

    Gesture software runs on a separate computer and sends commands over the local network to a USB receiver.

  2. 02

    Improve recognition

    Expand testing across presenters and camera positions, reduce missed and unintended commands, and improve responsiveness.

  3. 03

    Pilot and integrate

    Explore integration with an AV partner and test in presentation rooms. Use feedback to evaluate compatibility, installation and support before a wider release.
